# Code of Conduct

## Our pledge

We want Shobdo Keyboard to be a place where anyone can contribute — regardless of
experience, language, nationality, gender identity, disability, appearance, religion or
technical background. Bangla computing has a long history of volunteers sharing their work
generously, and this project follows that tradition.

## Expected behaviour

- Be kind and assume good intent. Most disagreements here are about transliteration edge
  cases, not about people.
- Give and accept constructive feedback gracefully. Critique the code, not the person.
- Respect that contributors may not write English or Bangla perfectly; never mock someone's
  language, spelling or grammar.
- Be patient with people asking basic questions. Everyone started somewhere.
- Accept a maintainer's decision once it is made, and feel free to argue the case before
  that point, with evidence.

## Unacceptable behaviour

- Harassment, insults, or personal attacks, including in issues, reviews and commit
  messages.
- Discriminatory jokes or language of any kind.
- Sexual attention or imagery, or unwelcome advances.
- Publishing someone's private information without permission.
- Deliberately derailing discussions, or sustained disruption.
- Political or religious campaigning unrelated to the project.

## Scope

This applies to all project spaces: issues, pull requests, reviews, discussions, and any
public space where someone is representing the project.

## Reporting

Report unacceptable behaviour privately to the maintainer via
<https://github.com/34rns> — do not open a public issue about it. Reports are handled
confidentially. We will respond as quickly as we reasonably can and explain what happens
next.

Maintainers may remove comments, close issues, or block accounts as needed. Consequences
escalate: a private warning, then a temporary ban, then a permanent ban for serious or
repeated behaviour.
